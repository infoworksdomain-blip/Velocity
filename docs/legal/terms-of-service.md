## The service

Velocity turns a website URL into short-form video/slideshow content and publishes it to TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts via each platform's **official API**, using your own connected accounts under your own OAuth authorization (C1). We never operate, provision, or trade social accounts on your behalf, and we never publish via unofficial/headless-browser methods.

## Your responsibilities

- You are responsible for the accuracy of the brand/product information you provide and for reviewing and approving content before it publishes (C7 — no AI-generated content publishes without your recorded approval).
- You are responsible for complying with each connected platform's own developer/creator terms.
- You must not use the service to generate content impersonating a real, named public figure without their consent (a real, enforced policy, not just a stated one — see our AI persona generation policy).

## AI-generated content and provenance

Every piece of AI-generated content carries a provenance record and, where the target platform supports it, a native AI-content disclosure label (C2, EU AI Act Art. 50). We never remove or forge this labeling.

## Fees and billing

Subscription and usage-based (credit) billing is processed via Stripe. See our current published pricing for plan details. Failed payments are subject to a grace period before service is suspended; see our billing FAQ for the current grace-period length.

## Data and content ownership

You retain ownership of your brand assets and the content generated for your workspace. We retain no license to your content beyond what's needed to operate the service (generation, storage, delivery to the platforms you connect).

## Termination

You may cancel at any time via your billing settings. We may suspend or terminate accounts that violate these terms, misuse the platform (including automated abuse — see our real, enforced rate-limiting and fraud-signal mechanisms), or violate a connected platform's own terms in a way that puts our platform-level API access at risk.
